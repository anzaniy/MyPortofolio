## Overview

Riverbend is a cozy guild-management demo. You play as the Guild Master: read request letters at the work desk, accept quests, and send adventurers out to run them while the guild hall is built and furnished.

## Features

- A morning to evening daily loop with running time and a quest queue at the reception desk.
- A quest announcement board with daily quests and standing orders, each showing power requirement, duration, client fee, and rewards.
- A build mode to lay out floors, walls, and guild facilities such as the clinic, gathering table, and waiting bench.
- Guild Master customization covering face, hair, hats, and clothing.
- Guild reputation that grows from quests and facilities, funded by gold and crafting materials.

## Status

Version 0.1 (Windows 64-bit) is available on itch.io. The project is still in development and more content is on the way.
