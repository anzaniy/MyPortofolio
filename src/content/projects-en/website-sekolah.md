## Overview

A school profile website for SMA Al Kautsar that serves as the school's official information hub. The project was built during an internship as a Web Developer Intern, from content gathering to page implementation.

## Background

School information such as the profile, history, alumni data, and activity documentation was scattered and not centralized. The school needed a single website that the public could access and the staff could easily update.

## My Role

- Designed and built the school website with **CodeIgniter 3**.
- Collected alumni data, school history, and activity documentation.
- Structured the pages so information is easy to find.
- Adjusted the layout so it reads comfortably on desktop and mobile.

## Features

- **School profile**: general information, vision, and mission.
- **Alumni data**: an alumni list as part of the school archive.
- **School history**: the school's journey and development.
- **Activity documentation**: photo galleries and records of school activities.

## Workflow

1. **Requirements gathering**: interviews and data collection with the school.
2. **Structure design**: defining pages and the navigation flow.
3. **Implementation**: building the pages with the CodeIgniter 3 MVC pattern.
4. **Testing**: checking the layout and core functions before handover.
