## Overview

Hunter Board is a fan-made reference site for **Monster Hunter Wilds** and **Monster Hunter Outlanders**. Its main focus is a quest board for comparing rewards, plus a character builder for putting weapon builds together. The whole site is built with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript: no framework, no backend, and no build step.

## Background

Quest reward information such as HRP, Zenny, and unique rewards is spread across various wiki pages, so players have to open many tabs just to compare a few quests. Hunter Board brings that data into one table that can be filtered, sorted, and searched right in the browser.

## My Role

- Designed the site structure and interface for two games in one hub.
- Compiled the quest, build, and skill datasets and the monster icon mapping.
- Implemented the quest table with filters, sorting, search, and pagination.
- Built the character builder along with its build detail panel.
- Set up the legal pages, metadata, sitemap, and deployment.

## Features

- **Quest board**: 155 quests (including event quests) with quest type filters, HRP sorting, search, and pagination.
- **Character builder**: 14 weapon builds complete with a detail panel and skill search.
- **Monster icons**: 56 monster icons and 14 weapon icons mapped automatically to monster names.
- **Two-game hub**: separate entry points for Wilds and Outlanders.
- **Support pages**: about, contact, privacy, credits, and a custom 404.
- **Search engine ready**: sitemap, robots.txt, and a 1200×630 social preview image.
