## Overview

A Visual Novel educational game covering **Plane Analytic Geometry**. Players follow the story while answering math questions along the way. The project was part of research at Lampung University, from design through testing.

## Background

Analytic Geometry is often considered difficult because it is abstract. A Visual Novel approach was chosen so the material is delivered through story and dialogue, making it easier to follow than a standard text module.

## My Role

- Built the game from start to finish using **Ren'Py**.
- Applied the **Game Development Life Cycle (GDLC)** methodology.
- Designed the storyline, dialogue, and subject questions.
- Ran **Alpha Testing** (internal functionality) and **Beta Testing** (user experience).
- Wrote the final research report.

## Features

- **Branching storyline**: dialogue choices affect how the story unfolds.
- **Subject questions**: Analytic Geometry problems embedded in the dialogue.
- **Answer feedback**: players get an explanation whether they answer right or wrong.
- **Visual and audio assets**: backgrounds, characters, and mood-setting music.

## GDLC Methodology

1. **Initiation**: defining learning goals and project constraints.
2. **Pre-production**: designing the story, material, and assets.
3. **Production**: implementing the scripts and systems in Ren'Py.
4. **Testing**: Alpha Testing for functionality, Beta Testing for player experience.
5. **Release**: preparing the build and the research report.
